      <h2>1. Introduction</h2>
      <p>
        This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with the mobile
        applications published on the Google Play Store under the developer name <b>Pocopie</b>
        (&ldquo;we&rdquo;, &ldquo;us&rdquo;, or &ldquo;our&rdquo;). It applies to every such
        application (each an &ldquo;App&rdquo;, and together &ldquo;our apps&rdquo;) unless a
        particular App links to its own separate privacy policy from its store listing. Pocopie is an
        independent app developer. Our apps are built with the Unity game engine.
      </p>
      <p>
        All of our apps share the same design principles. Each App runs entirely on your device: it
        does not require an account, a login, or an internet connection to use, and it contains no
        in-app purchases. We operate no servers, and no App sends information about you to us. Our
        apps are supported by advertising, which is delivered by Google AdMob and requires an
        internet connection to display.
      </p>
      <p>
        This policy applies only to our Android applications themselves. It does not apply to the
        Google Play Store, to advertisers whose ads may appear in an App, or to any website or
        service you may reach by tapping an ad. Those are governed by their own privacy policies.
      </p>
      <p>
        By installing and using any of our apps, you acknowledge the practices described in this
        policy. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the App.

      <div class="callout">
        <p>
          <b>Pocopie does not collect, store, or receive any personally identifiable information

    <section id="automatically-collected-data">
      <h2>3. Automatically Collected Data</h2>
      <p>
        To request, display, and measure advertisements, the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) software
        development kit integrated into the App may collect and transmit the following categories of
        data to Google:
      </p>
      <dl class="data-list">
        <div>
          <dt>Android Advertising ID (AAID)</dt>
          <dd>
            A resettable, pseudonymous identifier provided by Google Play services that is used to
            recognise your device for advertising purposes. It is not your name and is not
            permanently tied to you &mdash; you can reset or delete it at any time (see
            Section&nbsp;8).
          </dd>
        </div>
        <div>
          <dt>IP address and approximate (coarse) location</dt>
          <dd>
            Your device&rsquo;s IP address is visible whenever it connects to Google&rsquo;s ad
            servers, and a coarse location &mdash; typically at the level of a city, region, or
            country &mdash; may be derived from it. Our apps do not request Android&rsquo;s precise
            GPS location permission and do not access your exact location.
          </dd>
        </div>
        <div>
          <dt>Device and technical characteristics</dt>
          <dd>
            Device model and manufacturer, Android operating system version, device language and
            locale settings, screen size, resolution and orientation, network connection type, and
            similar technical attributes used to select and correctly render an ad.
          </dd>
        </div>
        <div>
          <dt>Ad interaction data</dt>
          <dd>
            Which ads were shown to you (impressions), whether you tapped them (clicks), how long an
            ad was viewed, whether a rewarded video was watched to completion, and whether an ad was
            closed or skipped.
          </dd>
        </div>
        <div>
          <dt>App usage events related to ad serving</dt>
          <dd>
            Limited in-app events tied to advertising, such as the App being opened or resumed, the
            point at which an ad request is made, which ad placement was involved (banner,
            interstitial, rewarded video, or app open), and the version of the App and the ads SDK
            in use.
          </dd>
        </div>
      </dl>
      <p>
        This data is collected by Google in its capacity as our advertising provider. We receive
        access only to aggregated, non-identifying reporting &mdash; for example, total impressions,
        total clicks, and estimated earnings for a given day or country. We cannot see, and are not
        given, the data of any individual user.
      </p>

    <section id="how-we-use-information">
      <h2>6. How We Use Information</h2>
      <p>
        Because we do not receive your personal data, the uses below describe what the data
        collected through AdMob is used for by Google on our behalf:
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>
          <b>Ad delivery.</b> Selecting and serving advertisements that are technically compatible
          with your device, network, and language.
        </li>
        <li>
          <b>Ad personalization.</b> Where you have given consent, showing ads that Google considers
          more relevant to you based on your advertising identifier and inferred interests. Without
          consent, ads are served on a non-personalized basis, using contextual signals rather than
          your interest profile.
        </li>
        <li>
          <b>Frequency capping.</b> Limiting how often the same ad is repeated to you and spacing
          out full-screen ads so the App stays usable.
        </li>
        <li>
          <b>Fraud and abuse prevention.</b> Detecting invalid traffic, automated clicks, and other
          abusive or fraudulent activity that would otherwise harm advertisers and the ad ecosystem.
        </li>
        <li>
          <b>Aggregate analytics and reporting.</b> Producing summary statistics &mdash; impressions,
          click-through rates, fill rates, and estimated revenue &mdash; that help us understand how
          each App performs overall and how to improve it. These reports are aggregated and do not
          identify individual users.
        </li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        We do not sell your personal information for money, we do not build profiles of users
        ourselves, and we do not use any of this data to contact you.
      </p>

          We have considered this processing against your rights and consider it proportionate and
          limited to what is necessary.
        </li>
      </ul>

    <section id="your-choices">
      <h2>8. Your Choices and Controls</h2>
      <p>
        You have meaningful control over the advertising data associated with your device. The exact
        menu names vary slightly between Android versions and manufacturers.
      </p>

      <h3>Reset or delete your Advertising ID</h3>
      <p>
        On your Android device, open <b>Settings &rarr; Google &rarr; All services (or Ads)
        &rarr; Ads</b>. From there you can:
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>
          <b>Reset advertising ID</b> &mdash; replaces your current identifier with a new one,
          disconnecting your device from any profile previously built against the old ID.
        </li>
        <li>
          <b>Delete advertising ID</b> &mdash; available on Android&nbsp;12 and later, this removes
          the identifier entirely. Apps requesting it receive a string of zeros, and personalized
          advertising based on it is no longer possible.
        </li>
      </ul>

      <h3>Opt out of ad personalization</h3>
      <p>
        The same Android <b>Ads</b> settings screen contains an option to opt out of ad
        personalization device-wide. You can also manage your preferences for your Google Account at
        <a href="https://adssettings.google.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">adssettings.google.com</a>.
        After opting out, you will still see ads in the App &mdash; that is how it stays free &mdash;
        but they will not be tailored to your interests.
      </p>

      <h3>Withdraw consent</h3>
      <p>
        Where a consent message was shown to you in the App, you can change or withdraw your choice
        through the privacy or consent option available in the App&rsquo;s settings, where provided.
        You may also withdraw consent by using the Android controls described above, which apply
        across all apps on your device, or by contacting us at the address in Section&nbsp;16. If you
        would prefer that no advertising data be collected from your device at all, the most complete
        option is to uninstall the App.
      </p>
      <p><a class="to-top" href="#main">&uarr; Back to top</a></p>
    </section>

        knowingly collect personal information from children.
      </p>
      <p>
        We comply with the United States <b>Children&rsquo;s Online Privacy Protection Act
        (COPPA)</b>. Under <b>Article&nbsp;8 of the GDPR</b>, the processing of a child&rsquo;s
